TRANSPARENCY

What this prototype does — and does not do

Rules cover two specific domestic UPI failure categories from RBI's 2019 TAT framework. Other or unverified cases are sent to human review. The delay amount is an indicative calculation, not confirmed liability, unpaid compensation or savings. Resolution date means concluded reversal or confirmed beneficiary credit for fund transfers. The applicable institution must verify compensation and its recipient; qualifying compensation is proactive.

All cases are generated for demonstration. The fixed cohort is a snapshot as of 23 November 2026. Later review dates assume no new resolutions or events; this is a what-if projection, not historical or live reporting. No real customer data, transaction IDs, credentials or bank API connections are used.
